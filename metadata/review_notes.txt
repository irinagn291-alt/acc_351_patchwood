Patchwood opens directly to Basecamp without onboarding or an account. The interface and course content are in English.

To review, open the available mission, read the briefing and start its three challenges. Select or assemble an answer and check it; incorrect answers can be retried. Complete each challenge correctly to reach the completion screen. Close a mission partway through and reopen it to check checkpoint restoration. Use the bottom bar to inspect the Trail map and Field log. Completed missions can be replayed; subsequent missions unlock in sequence.

Progress and completion dates are stored locally in UserDefaults. There is no populated in-app demo mode. The reviewed project declares no protected-resource permissions and implements no network, account, analytics, advertising, purchase or third-party service integrations. Successful checks use system sensory feedback.

This information is based on source and configuration review; no build, simulator or device was run.
